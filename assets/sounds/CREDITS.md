# Sound credits

All sounds that come with the app may be shipped in it: they are either made
for it or released as CC0 (public domain). Credit is not required by CC0 but
given here anyway.

The CC0 sounds that came as Ogg files were converted to MP3 (ffmpeg,
VBR quality 2), because Windows cannot play Ogg.
