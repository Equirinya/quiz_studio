Quiz Studio

Host your own quiz show. Build quizzes with friends, put them on the big screen and run the evening from your phone.

Run the show from your phone

Open questions, reveal answers, give points and play sounds. Right, Wrong and Reveal always sit in the same place, so you never have to look for them.

Every phone is a buzzer

Teams scan a code and their phone becomes their buzzer. No account needed, and it works over the local Wi-Fi even without internet. USB buzzer boxes work too.

Answers on the teams' devices

Whiteboards, numbers, sliders, pick one, put in order and match pairs. You show the answers one by one and rate them, many rate themselves.

Puzzle rounds

Find four groups of four, match the pairs or race through quick-fire rounds, with animations on every device and the big screen.

Survey rounds and more

Guess the most popular answers to a survey, picture and clue reveals, music, confetti, fireworks and cards with pictures or video over the screen.

Build quizzes together

Invite co-authors with a link and share single categories with them, so nobody spoils the rest of the evening.
