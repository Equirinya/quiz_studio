# Delete Your Account and Data

## In the app

Open the app, tap the account icon at the top right, then **Delete account**. Accounts with an email address confirm with their password. The account is deleted right away.

## What is deleted
- Your sign-in and your profile (your name)
- Games only you are in, with their questions, pictures and sounds
- The quiz sessions you host

Games you share with others stay with them, including the questions you wrote there. If you were a game's only admin, one of its co-authors becomes admin. This cannot be undone.

## By email

If you can no longer open the app, send us an email instead and we will delete the account for you.

## Send Deletion Request
